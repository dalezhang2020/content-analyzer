# Content Analyzer — Product Definition

## What it is

A local workbench for analyzing social-media content (YouTube, Xiaohongshu). Paste a URL, watch the pipeline run, read a structured teardown.

## Register

**Product** — a focused tool for creators who study content patterns. Not a dashboard, not a SaaS, not an AI demo. It is a research desk.

## Core flow

1. User pastes a URL (YouTube or Xiaohongshu).
2. Pipeline runs: fetch → extract → analyze → report.
3. Results appear in a clean, editorial layout: metadata, teardown fields, image analysis, markdown report, raw JSON.

## Audience

Solo creators and small teams who reverse-engineer successful content to inform their own.

## Non-goals

- Multi-user collaboration
- Cloud deployment (local-first)
- Real-time streaming analytics
- Social login or accounts
